Profile

At Peddle Thorp PNG, every project demands a response to its place.

Peddle Thorp Architects PNG has practised in Port Moresby for more than thirty years and is recognised as one of the significant practices in Papua New Guinea's architecture industry. We are associated with Peddle Thorp in Queensland, with sixty years of design experience there, and with an international Peddle Thorp group.

We are driven by a passion for making excellent architecture a normal part of everyday development. We design around the human experience, plan every project to return value and grow with its owners, and protect the natural environment while improving the built one.

Our studio works in joint venture with Glen Petersen Architect. Clients include the National Judiciary Services, Bank South Pacific, Westpac, Kumul Holdings, Puma Energy, the Asian Development Bank and the World Bank.

Architecture

We design buildings that stand the test of time: courts, banks, offices and public buildings shaped by PNG's climate and culture. Every brief starts with understanding how the building will be used and by whom.

Services: project brief preparation, site analysis and feasibility, design and documentation.

Interior

Workplace and branch interiors for banks, holding companies and international organisations, including Bank South Pacific, Westpac, Kumul Holdings, the Asian Development Bank and the World Bank.

Planning

Masterplanning and facility planning

Long-range plans for campuses, estates and sites that will grow in stages, and space planning for courts, banks and offices, so each stage adds value and nothing has to be undone.

Project delivery

Contract administration and project coordination from tender to practical completion. One point of contact for engineers, consultants and contractors, with the backing of our Queensland studios on large projects.

Sustainability

Papua New Guinea's heat, rain and seismic activity shape every drawing. Our approach draws on the way PNG has always built, and on current tropical design practice.

Air

Cross ventilation

Buildings oriented to catch the prevailing breeze, cutting reliance on air conditioning.

Shade

Deep roofs and screens

Wide eaves, verandahs and screens keep out sun and monsoon rain.

Ground

Raised and resilient

Raised floors and robust structure for flood, termite and earthquake conditions.

Local

Local materials and skills

Materials and trades available in PNG, so buildings can be maintained for decades.

Overall DuluxGroup Design Excellence Award winner 2026

Celebrating architecture, design and colour in PNG! More than 400 guests came together at the Hilton Port Moresby for the return of the PNGIA Dulux Design Excellence Awards, celebrating 11 awards across design, heritage and colour.

After a long break since before COVID, it was fantastic to once again celebrate the creativity, innovation and cultural identity shaping Papua New Guinea’s built environment.

A special congratulations to Peddle Thorp Asia Pacific, named the Overall DuluxGroup Design Excellence Award winner, along with all our 2026 finalists and award winners. The future is bright. Together, let’s colour the future of Papua New Guinea.

Project news

Waigani National Courts Complex officially opened

Governor-General Grand Chief Sir Bob Dadae opened the new courts complex on 10 September 2025, during PNG’s 50th Independence celebrations. Prime Minister James Marape called it “an investment in the very core of our democracy”. The complex has 15 courtrooms, including the Banco Court, and 31 judges’ chambers.
